Three quiet designs for a precise studio.

Each proposal below is a complete, standalone landing page for MicroAxis — a precision software studio ("micro": atomic detail; "axis": directional clarity). All three share one quotation engine that reads a visitor's answers, prices the build across Standard, Premium, and Elegant tiers, and detects specialised add-ons. What changes between them is the visual language and the way the form behaves.

Direction: contemporary minimalism — type-led, generous margins, two or three colors, motion only in response to the visitor. The estimate is presented as a banded range plus an itemised figure, per current pricing-UX research.

The shared quotation engine

Five inputs — product type, scope, launch scale, timeline, specialised add-ons — produce a complexity score. The score selects a recommended tier; the estimate is computed inside that tier's band and itemised on screen. Band ranges are fixed for trust; the computed figure is explained line by line.
